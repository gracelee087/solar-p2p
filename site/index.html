Simulation: fictional neighbourhood in Mainz & Wiesbaden, real German day-ahead prices (SMARD) and real irradiance (Open-Meteo), June 2026. Payments on Solana devnet in a test-EUR token. Tariffs are assumptions: grid price 35 ct/kWh, feed-in 7 ct; shared energy still pays grid fees, levies and taxes (assumed 20 ct/kWh, see Bundesnetzagentur), so only the energy part moves between neighbours.
